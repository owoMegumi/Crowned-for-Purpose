About the Foundation

Who We Are

Founded in February 2026, Crowned for Purpose was born from a genuine heart for people and a calling to see women and girls discover their identity in Christ.

We believe every girl and woman deserves to be seen, valued, treated with dignity, and encouraged to embrace her God-given purpose. Through compassionate outreach, practical care drives, and Christ-centred fellowship, we actively meet practical needs while fostering spiritual growth.
